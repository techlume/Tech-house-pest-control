PIGEON
 

Bird pest control focuses on humane deterrents and exclusion methods rather than lethal measures, as many wild bird species are legally protected. Pest birds like pigeons, sparrows, starlings, and seagulls cause property damage through corrosive droppings (uric acid), blocked drains, and airborne pathogens (histoplasmosis).
 
1. Physical Exclusion & Barrier Methods
Physical barriers are the most reliable long-term solution because they physically prevent birds from landing, roosting, or nesting.
Primary Exclusion SolutionsAnti-Bird Netting: Heavy-duty polyethylene mesh installed over balconies, courtyards, HVAC units, and warehouse rafters. It completely seals off large areas.
 
Bird Spikes: Stainless steel or polycarbonate strips attached to ledges, window sills, parapets, and roof edges to prevent landing without harming the birds.
Bird Wire Systems: Spring-tensioned stainless steel wires installed along narrow ledges, create an unstable landing platform that deters medium-to-large birds.
2. Visual, Tactile & Audio DeterrentsSecondary deterrents work by scaring birds or creating uncomfortable surface textures. They work best when combined with physical barriers.
 
Tactile Gels & Optical Gel Cups: Non-toxic, sticky or reflective gels placed on ledges. Optical cups emit UV light patterns that appear as flame or smoke to birds.
Visual Scare Devices: Reflective scare tape, holographic diverters, or predator decoys (e.g., motion-activated owls or falcons). Note: Move static decoys regularly to prevent birds from becoming accustomed to them.
 
Sonic & Ultrasonic Repellers: Audio devices broadcasting predator calls or distress signals to disperse flocks from open yards, orchards, or industrial rooftops.
